Ninety-nine Names, one sweep of the second hand

The analog face is the same clock as in the app. Each second lights the next Name. Allah sits at the centre. Grey names have already passed this minute.

Duas

Sourced duas, on a schedule

Twenty minutes after Fajr a dua from the list is sent as a notice. On Friday at Dhuhr the app reminds you to read Surat al-Kahf. Routine items can be duas you pick. Nothing is invented. Each card cites its source.

Island

A light pill over other apps

On Android, allow display over other apps. The island stays a small pill until you touch it. If you do not, it waits three seconds, then folds back. Windows has the same island window.
