ASMA CLOCK - deploy notes (for you only)

DEPLOY ON NETLIFY
Git: connect the GitHub repo. Publish directory must be "web" (netlify.toml sets this).
If Site configuration still has Publish directory empty or ".", set it to web and trigger a redeploy.
Drop: drag the "web" folder (the one containing index.html), not the whole repo.
3. Open the site link on your phone in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
   Android: menu > Install app / Add to Home screen.
   iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen.
It must be served over https, which Netlify does by default. Location, notifications,
install and offline mode all need https.

WHAT IT DOES
- Dial: 99 names around the ring, the hand does one lap every 60 seconds, passed names turn grey,
  the current one is highlighted. Hour and minute hands sit in the centre.
- Under the dial: digital time (Arabic numerals), Gregorian and Hijri date.
- The big name below changes every minute and cycles through all 99 in order
  (minutes since midnight, modulo 99). Settings > "Name shown below the dial" can switch it to
  the name under the hand.
- Prayer times are calculated on the device (no internet needed after first load).
  Default method is Muslim World League. UOIF, Egyptian, Karachi, ISNA and Umm al-Qura are in Settings.
- Location is detected automatically. It is rechecked every new day and whenever you reopen the app,
  and the times are recalculated. You can also pick a city or tap Update my location.
- Imsak, Suhoor, Iftar countdown, sunnah fast day tags (Mon, Thu, white days, Arafah, Ashura),
  Ramadan is detected automatically.
- Hijri calendar and Gregorian calendar with Islamic dates, prayer times for any selected day.
- Routine tab: pre-built items (wake for suhoor, Fajr, adhkar, Quran, Duha, five prayers, Iftar,
  Witr, sleep). Switch on what you want, edit times, add your own.
- Duas tab: 27 daily duas with Arabic, English, French, sources, plus all 99 names with search.
- Languages: English, French, Arabic (right to left).

LIMITS TO KNOW
- It installs as an app icon, not a true home screen widget.
- Alerts and adhan sound work while the app is open or recently in the background.
  Browsers do not let a web app wake itself when closed or with the screen locked.
  Notifications are shown if the app is still alive in the background.
- Sound only plays after you have tapped on the page once.
- No adhan recording is included (rights). In Settings > Adhan sound > Choose audio file you can
  pick your own mp3, it is stored on the phone.
- Hijri dates can differ by one day from your local moon sighting announcement.
  Use Settings > Hijri date adjustment (-2 to +2).
- The Hijri day changes at Maghrib by default (Settings can turn that off).
- Prayer times use the phone's own timezone setting.

UPDATING
After you upload a new version, open the app twice so the new files replace the cached ones.
If you change files, edit CACHE = 'asma-v1' in sw.js to 'asma-v2' to force the refresh.
